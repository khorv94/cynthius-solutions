Meet the Founder

Kirsten Horvath founded Cynthius Solutions as a natural evolution of her personal and professional experience. With a background in web development and IT support, Kirsten brings a business-minded approach to every new client project.

Kirsten graduated from the University of Wisconsin-Milwaukee with a B.S. in Business Administration: Information Technology Management. She spent over a decade perfecting her IT skills, working with clients and managers across different areas of technology to solve complex problems with practical solutions.

That blend of business savvy and technical experience shaped the creation of Cynthius Solutions. No website is viewed as a “one and done” project. Each is examined through the lens of where a client is now, what framework will support their operations, and where they are going in the future.

As the primary developer at Cynthius Solutions, Kirsten often works directly with clients throughout the entire development process. This includes everything from the customer-facing aspects of a layout to hosting, content updates, maintenance, and long-term technical support. When a project requires additional expertise, she can collaborate with known marketing and design professionals to bring the right set of skills to the project without necessarily expanding its scope.

Most importantly, Cynthius Solutions is about long-term client relationships. Each unique website is built to complement the customer's goals, growing and evolving with business changes and personal journeys. Learn more about this process by contacting Cynthius Solutions directly.
