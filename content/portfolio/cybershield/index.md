## Introduction

A technology company has to evolve quickly. New products and threats are around every corner of the online world. That means the website has to provide an engaging format with professional reassurance, as well as the opportunity to scale when new services are required.

### The Challenge

A lot of technical knowledge goes into IT and cybersecurity website design. It’s not like typical business website development, because the language and presentation matter for effective visitor engagement, especially when seeking leads. The challenge is to showcase what services clients need early and to ensure proper speed and responsiveness to stand out.

### The Solution

Cynthius Solutions planned CyberShield’s IT services website development around Hugo and Tailwind CSS. This ensured a more responsive design that has customization at every level. The technology company website has semantic HTML, SEO, and caching on the front end and analytics support on the back end, so the brand can maintain control over visuals while expanding services.
