## Introduction

There are between 28 and 31 million e-commerce sites operating worldwide. That can put any newcomer at a serious disadvantage. To rise to the top, modern digital stores require e-commerce website development blending engaging visuals that attract consumers with technical functionality for faster product presentation and transactions.

### The Challenge

The online store development goal of Adrian was to both provide attractive clothing product pages and make it easier for customers to navigate to any specific item or collection. There has to be a simple way to interact with the website that isn’t weighed down by backend functionality.

### The Solution

Cynthius Solutions developed a custom e-commerce website for Adrian using Hugo. That included bringing in a SnipCart integration for essential digital shopping functionality, as well as adding Fuse.js for site search so customers could quickly find specific products. To speed up the site, active caching, optimized images, universal CSS, and JavaScript were implemented.

With Adrian, the vibrant colors and simplified navigation lay the foundation for our e-commerce web design. Products can be quickly discovered, and a holistic visual presentation brings the entire site together.
