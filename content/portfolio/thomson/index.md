## Introduction

Everyone from photographers to IT consultants needs portfolio web design. Having a professional way to present what you can do and how it can address clients’ needs is essential for generating more prospective leads. It’s also a powerful way to showcase art projects, clothing designs, and other personal endeavors.

### The Challenge

Most personal portfolio websites have to walk a fine line between making a solid impression on visitors and keeping the focus on the actual work being presented. The trick was having a polished digital presence that included structure for communication, without reducing any of the individual project details.

### The Solution

Cynthius Solutions chose a minimalist yet professional portfolio website. Thomson uses a visually focused approach with a 6-page structure, so there is more than enough room to reinforce branding without sacrificing the attention each project deserves.
