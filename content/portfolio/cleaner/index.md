## Cleaner Project

**_Local Service Business Website Development_**

Search for any “cleaner service near me” inquiry on Google, and you’ll be flooded with responses. There is often extremely high demand in any service industry, including home and business cleaners. To meet that demand, a responsive service business website with plenty of white space is needed.

### The Challenge

Most cities have a long list of cleaning crews, local plumbers, pest control experts, and similar service-based companies. The first impression a prospective client has when visiting a service provider's website makes a big difference in lead generation. The challenge is keeping everything organized while ensuring the site is up to date for business changes.

### The Solution

Cynthius Solutions created Cleaner as an example of what a 9+ small business web development project could look like. It uses both Hugo and Tailwind CSS, with Markdown or MDX as the CMS for content updates. The clean structure ensures readers navigate an SEO-friendly architecture that explains the who, what, where, why, and how the company can address client needs.

Cleaner is an excellent example of what modern local business website design can do for startups and veteran companies alike. It provides a speedy framework with responsive functionality to bring any business into the modern era, helping establish a more professional image.
