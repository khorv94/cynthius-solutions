## Introduction

Plenty of professionals in the online world are content or media creators. Everyone from artists to YouTube personalities. Each needs a portfolio website design that builds strong imagery and offers flexible viewing options across devices, while organizing content in a way that aligns with the creator’s skills and ideas.

### The Challenge

With Nebula, the primary challenge was finding a professional portfolio website structure that supported heavy image usage. There could be no clutter, so clients could naturally move from engaging visuals to the written content, contact information, or credentials, as if flowing through a gallery.

### The Solution

Cynthius Solutions formed Nebula as a more visually focused creative portfolio website. Many of the bases are image-based, using prominent cover media. To meet the gallery's needs, light and dark viewing modes were integrated, providing guests with more flexibility and visual contrast to distinguish different posts, elements, or pieces.
