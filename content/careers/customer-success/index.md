### Job Description

We are looking for a friendly and customer-focused Customer Success Representative to join our team. In this role, you will serve as the primary point of contact for existing clients, ensuring they receive exceptional support and ongoing value from our services. You will help maintain strong client relationships, address questions or concerns, and coordinate with internal teams to ensure client satisfaction and long-term success.

The ideal candidate is an excellent communicator who enjoys helping people, solving problems, and building positive relationships. If you are passionate about customer experience and digital services, we’d love to hear from you.

##### Responsibilities:

* Build and maintain strong relationships with existing clients;

* Respond to client questions, requests, and support inquiries in a timely and professional manner;

* Coordinate with design, development, and project management teams to resolve client issues;

* Monitor client accounts and ensure ongoing satisfaction with agency services;

* Provide guidance and recommendations to help clients achieve their website and business goals;

* Schedule and conduct client check-ins, follow-ups, and review meetings;

* Assist clients with website updates, service renewals, and onboarding processes;

* Track client feedback and identify opportunities to improve customer experience;

* Maintain accurate records of client communications and support requests;

##### Requirements:

* Proven experience in Customer Success, Customer Support, Account Management, or a related role;

* Strong communication and interpersonal skills;

* Excellent problem-solving abilities and attention to detail;

* Ability to manage multiple client accounts and priorities simultaneously;

* Experience using CRM or customer support tools such as HubSpot, Zendesk, Freshdesk, or similar platforms;

* Basic understanding of websites, digital marketing, or web design services is preferred;

* Ability to work collaboratively with cross-functional teams;

* Strong organizational and time management skills;

* A positive, professional, and customer-first attitude;
