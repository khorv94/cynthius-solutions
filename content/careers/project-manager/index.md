### Job Description

We are seeking an organized and proactive Project Manager to join our web design agency team. In this role, you will oversee the planning, coordination, and execution of website and digital projects from concept to completion. You will work closely with designers, developers, clients, and stakeholders to ensure projects are delivered on time, within budget, and aligned with client expectations.

The ideal candidate is an excellent communicator with strong leadership and problem-solving skills, capable of managing multiple projects in a fast-paced creative environment. If you are passionate about digital projects, client success, and team collaboration, we’d love to hear from you.

##### Responsibilities:

* Manage website projects from initial planning through final delivery;

* Communicate with clients to understand project goals, timelines, and requirements;

* Coordinate with designers, developers, and marketing teams to ensure smooth project execution;

* Create project timelines, schedules, and task assignments;

* Monitor project progress and ensure deadlines, budgets, and quality standards are met;

* Identify and resolve project risks, issues, and bottlenecks efficiently;

* Conduct client meetings, provide regular project updates, and maintain strong client relationships;

* Review deliverables to ensure they align with client expectations and agency standards;

* Assist in improving internal workflows, processes, and project management systems;

##### Requirements:

* Proven experience as a Project Manager, Account Manager, or similar role in a web design, creative, or digital agency;

* Excellent communication, leadership, and organizational skills;

* Experience using project management tools such as Trello, Asana, ClickUp, Monday.com, or Jira;

* Ability to manage multiple projects and priorities simultaneously;

* Strong problem-solving skills and attention to detail;

* Ability to work collaboratively with cross-functional creative and technical teams;

* Knowledge of UI/UX, web development, and digital marketing concepts is a plus;

* Bachelor’s degree in Business, Marketing, Communications, or a related field preferred;
