### Job Description

We are looking for a creative and detail-oriented Web Designer to join our team. In this role, you will be responsible for designing visually appealing, user-friendly websites and digital experiences that align with client goals and brand identity. You will collaborate closely with developers, marketing teams, and clients to create responsive and engaging web designs that deliver exceptional user experiences.

The ideal candidate has a strong eye for design, a solid understanding of UI/UX principles, and the ability to turn concepts into functional and attractive web layouts. If you are passionate about creativity, innovation, and digital design, we’d love to hear from you.

##### Responsibilities:

- Meet with clients or project managers to discuss website goals, requirements, and design concepts;

- Create wireframes, mockups, and responsive website layouts for desktop and mobile devices;

- Develop creative design solutions that enhance user experience and brand consistency;

- Collaborate with developers to ensure accurate implementation of web designs;

- Optimize website designs for usability, accessibility, and performance;

- Present design ideas, concepts, and final deliverables to clients or internal teams;

- Stay updated on current web design trends, tools, and best practices;

- Manage multiple design projects while meeting deadlines and quality standards;

##### Requirements:

- Proven experience as a Web Designer or similar creative role;

- Proficiency in design tools such as Figma, Adobe Creative Suite, Sketch, or Canva;

- Strong understanding of UI/UX design principles and responsive web design;

- Basic knowledge of HTML, CSS, and web development practices;

- Excellent visual design skills with attention to typography, color, and layout;

- Ability to communicate ideas clearly and collaborate effectively with teams and clients;

- Strong time management and organizational skills;

- A portfolio showcasing previous web design projects and creative work;
