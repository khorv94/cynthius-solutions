### Job Description

We are seeking a creative and results-driven SEO & Marketing Specialist to join our web design agency team. In this role, you will be responsible for developing and executing digital marketing strategies that improve online visibility, generate leads, and support client growth. You will work closely with designers, developers, and content teams to optimize websites and marketing campaigns for search engines and user engagement.

The ideal candidate has a strong understanding of SEO, digital marketing trends, analytics, and content strategy. If you are passionate about helping businesses grow online through data-driven marketing and optimization, we’d love to hear from you.

##### Responsibilities:

* Develop and implement SEO strategies to improve website rankings and organic traffic;

* Perform keyword research, competitor analysis, and on-page SEO optimization;

* Monitor website performance using tools such as Google Analytics, Google Search Console, and SEO platforms;

* Create and manage digital marketing campaigns across search engines, email, and social media platforms;

* Collaborate with designers and developers to optimize website structure, speed, and user experience for SEO;

* Write or coordinate SEO-friendly website content, blogs, and marketing copy;

* Track campaign performance and prepare reports with insights and recommendations;

* Stay updated on SEO algorithms, digital marketing trends, and industry best practices;

* Assist with lead generation, branding, and overall marketing strategy initiatives;

##### Requirements:

* Proven experience in SEO, digital marketing, or a related role;

* Strong understanding of on-page, off-page, and technical SEO principles;

* Experience with SEO and marketing tools such as Google Analytics, SEMrush, Ahrefs, Moz, or similar platforms;

* Knowledge of content marketing, email marketing, and social media strategies;

* Basic understanding of HTML, website structure, and web performance optimization;

* Excellent analytical, organizational, and communication skills;

* Ability to manage multiple campaigns and projects simultaneously;

* Creative thinking with a data-driven approach to marketing decisions;

* Bachelor’s degree in Marketing, Communications, Business, or a related field preferred;
